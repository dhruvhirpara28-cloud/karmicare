### The five changes that clear the most held text

1. **Practitioner block (6 entries, 476 words, 32 % of the held words).** Remove the nutritional-therapist quote (C84/C85); cut "The best outcome is the one prevented." and the "twelve hundred brushings" count from C70; get Leen Ponet's approval of her quote (C83; its clinical content is fine).
2. **Research cards (22 entries, 29 % of the held entries).** Keep the sleep card as worded with a footnote (C61); turn the fluoride card into a study-attributed sensitivity card (C63); rewrite the bottom text without "38,000" and "top of the evidence hierarchy" (C68). Then decide the other three cards: hide the 98 % card until its source is in the library (C60); remove the xylitol-caries and canker-sore cards, or keep them only with a regulatory decision on disease endpoints (C62, C64).
3. **Homepage benefits, FAQ and PDP comparison table (15 entries, 269 words).** Use the reworded lines for C21, C23, C24, C33, C37, C43, C44, C45, C46; replace the gum card (C35) and the focus/calm card (C40/C41) with the composition lines in §2; remove the oral-gut-brain row (C42); drop "settle gums" (C34).
4. **Leave-on duration and treatment journey (8 entries, 140 words).** Replace "works all night", "keeps working while you sleep" and "hours … instead of seconds" with the approved Leave-on wording (C10, C11); drop the week headings of the treatment journey and keep untimed lines (C94–C97), with C98 replaced. This also clears the onboarding lines C14, C94 and C97.
5. **Chronobiology clock (9 entries, 109 words).** Keep the Neisseria and Fusobacterium badges with a footnote (C50, C51); trim the night and morning texts (C52, C53); trim clock messages 1, 6 and 7 and hold 2–5 until a cariology source is in the library (C54).
